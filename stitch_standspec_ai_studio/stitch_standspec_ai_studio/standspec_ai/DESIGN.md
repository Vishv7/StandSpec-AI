## Brand & Style

The design system establishes an institutional regulatory assurance environment that projects unyielding precision, legal authority, and serene clarity. Built for compliance officers, legal counsels, and standards auditors navigating complex statutory frameworks (such as Quality Control Orders and international standards specifications), the interface rejects superficial decoration in favor of high-legibility typographic discipline, structured elevation, and analytical calm.

The overarching aesthetic is **Corporate / Modern** elevated by precision engineering cues:
- **Tone:** Authoritative, clinical, dependable, and rigorously organized.
- **Surface Integrity:** Bright, clinical white card layers suspended over soft architectural slate canvases, providing distinct spatial grouping without visual fatigue.
- **Assurance Color Logic:** Semantic indicators act as verified legal states rather than decorative accents. Crisp emerald signifies verified compliance and harmonized standards; amber highlights pending statutory Quality Control Orders (QCO) and audit warnings; technical blue directs interactive focus and analytical tooling.

## Colors

The palette is engineered for institutional review workflows requiring extended focus and instant semantic validation across dense standard specifications.

- **Primary (`#059669` - Compliance Emerald):** Applied to active compliance certifications, verified standard alignments, passed audit validations, and primary positive confirmation actions.
- **Secondary (`#2563EB` - Tech Blue):** Drives digital analytical tooling, interactive references, link navigations, active system filters, and focused states.
- **Tertiary (`#D97706` - Statutory Amber):** Reserved for statutory obligations, mandatory QCO milestones, pending approvals, critical advisories, and conditional non-conformance.
- **Neutral Foundation:**
  - `Canvas Ground`: `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50) establishes the primary backdrop.
  - `Sub-surface / Structural Wells`: `#F1F5F9` (Slate 100) provides recession for table headers, inactive segments, and side navigation.
  - `Raised Panels & Cards`: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White) creates clean, readable contrast planes.
  - `Dividers & Outlines`: `#E2E8F0` (Slate 200) for razor-sharp, low-friction structural demarcations.
  - `Typography`: Deep Slate (`#0F172A`) for authoritative display and headlines; Muted Slate (`#334155` and `#64748B`) for body text, meta annotations, and audit histories.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy harmonizes executive authority, document legibility, and technical specificity through three dedicated typefaces:

- **Manrope (Headlines):** Geometrically balanced, contemporary, and institutional. Its open counters and geometric proportion project structured governance and modern regulatory leadership.
- **Inter (Body & Narrative):** The benchmark for high-density document reading and data inspection. Used for statutory clause descriptions, audit notes, and long-form compliance records.
- **JetBrains Mono (Identifiers & Standards Metadata):** Implements absolute precision for ISO/IEC clause references, QCO gazette notification codes, timestamp logs, and audit hashing tags.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a 12-column fluid grid system engineered for wide-display compliance analytics while retaining strict vertical and horizontal rhythm.

- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12-column structure with `margin: 2rem` (32px) and `gutter: 1.5rem` (24px). Accommodates dual-panel layouts: pinned document clause navigators on the left, deep audit inspection workspaces on the right.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout collapsing secondary telemetry into contextual sliding drawers; standardizes on `gutter: 1.25rem` and `margin: 1.5rem`.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column reflow where multi-column regulatory tables shift into stacked summary cards; margin drops to `1rem` with `gutter-mobile: 1rem`.
- **Spacing Rhythm:** Built upon an intentional 4px/8px incremental cadence. Spacing values maintain generous internal padding within data cards to prevent cognitive clutter during multi-standard evaluations.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on structural tonal separation combined with delicate, low-opacity ambient shadows:

- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Flat `#F8FAFC`. Houses background canvas and non-interactive layout containers.
- **Level 1 (Structural Cards & Data Panels):** Pure `#FFFFFF` resting on `#F8FAFC`, delimited by a 1px border (`#E2E8F0`) and an ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Modules & Flyouts):** Used for elevated audit actions, active filtering popovers, and selected standard clauses. Border remains `#E2E8F0` with `box-shadow: 0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Statutory Resolution Overlays):** `#FFFFFF` bounded by `#CBD5E1` with `box-shadow: 0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.

## Shapes

The design system implements a **Soft** corner philosophy (`roundedness: 1`). 

- Default elements (inputs, badges, small buttons) feature `0.25rem` (4px) corner radii.
- Medium components (cards, nested panels, data tables) use `0.5rem` (8px).
- Modals, prominent audit containers, and command palettes employ `0.75rem` (12px).
- Pure pill shapes (`rounded-full`) are strictly reserved for verified compliance status chips, regulatory state pills, and analytical tags to differentiate dynamic statuses from actionable square UI controls.
