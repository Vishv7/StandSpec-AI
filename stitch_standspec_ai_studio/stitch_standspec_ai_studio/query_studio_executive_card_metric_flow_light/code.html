Match Rationale: The queried technical parameters specify a multi-core crosslinked polyethylene cable operating at 1.1 kV using solid/stranded aluminium conductor. Under the Indian Standards codex, IS 7098 (Part 1) explicitly governs all XLPE insulated cables up to 1100 V. The aluminium conductor is governed subordinately by IS 8130:2013 (Class 2), as cross-referenced in Clause 5.1.

policy Mandatory Tender Compliance for Public Procurement:
Under Section 16 of the Bureau of Indian Standards Act, 2016, products conforming to Gazette S.O. 4312(E) must bear the Standard ISI Mark. Any tenderer submitting bids without a certified BIS License (CM/L Number) is subject to summary disqualification at Technical Evaluation Stage without right of cure. Public sector procuring entities (CPWD, GeM, NHPC, State Discoms) are statutorily barred from procuring non-marked cables.
